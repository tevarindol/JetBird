JetBird is a wrapper around the NetBird client and is privacy friendly, containing no blobs or trackers.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Native UI</li>
<li>Supports SSO and setup keys</li>
<li>Supports pre-shared keys</li>
<li>Real-time logs</li>
<li>Quick tile</li>
<li>Exclude apps from the tunnel</li>
<li>Exit nodes (with select and client-side customisation)</li>
<li>Android TV support</li>
<li>Lazy connection support</li>
<li>Profiles</li>
<li>SOCKS5 mode</li>
</ul>
